Equipment we supply & support

Absorption heat pumps — low grade & high grade.

Systems we specify for your heat source and duty, then commission and support for life. Upgrade low-grade waste heat into useful process heat and district heating flow temperatures.

Low grade

Low Grade Heat Pump

Combines a high-temperature driving source with low-grade waste heat to deliver a large flow of medium-temperature heat. Typical COP around 1.7 — roughly 1.7 kW of useful heat for every 1 kW of driving heat.

  • Output tempUp to ~90 °C
  • Typical COP1.6 – 1.8
  • Best forDistrict heating, flue gas recovery
High grade

High Grade Heat Pump

Takes medium-temperature waste heat and lifts a portion 30–50 °C higher with almost no external energy input. Ideal where process waste heat exists but higher-grade heat is needed.

  • Temperature lift+30 – 50 °C
  • Typical COP≈ 0.45 – 0.5
  • Best forProcess industry upgrades

Where absorption heat pumps deliver value.

District heating networks

Recover flue gas and low-grade sources and lift them to network flow temperatures.

Upgrade waste streams for wash water, preheat and higher-temperature duties.

Data centre heat export

Lift server exhaust heat to temperatures neighbouring heat networks can use.

CHP & energy centres

Maximise the useful heat extracted from engines and boilers year-round.
