Absorption chillers

Absorption chillers — cooling powered by heat, not electricity.

LiBr/water absorption chillers use your waste heat, hot water, steam or exhaust gas as the driving energy — delivering chilled water with a fraction of the electrical demand of conventional chillers.

Most popular

Hot Water Driven

Single and double-effect machines driven by hot water from CHP, district heating, solar thermal or process waste heat.

  • Driving heat80–180 °C
  • Capacity100 kW – 12 MW
  • Typical COP0.7 – 1.4

Steam Driven

Double-effect steam-fired machines for sites with existing steam infrastructure — high efficiency cooling from process steam.

  • Driving heatSteam 1–10 bar
  • Capacity200 kW – 15 MW
  • Typical COP1.2 – 1.45

Exhaust & Direct-Fired

Fired by engine or turbine exhaust, or by gas burner — ideal for trigeneration and off-grid cooling.

  • Driving heatExhaust 250–550 °C
  • Capacity150 kW – 10 MW
  • Typical COP1.2 – 1.4
Why absorption cooling

Cut electrical cooling load dramatically.

Because the compression work is done thermally, an absorption chiller draws only a few kilowatts for its pumps. Where waste heat is available, cooling is produced almost for free — with water as the refrigerant, no F-gas burden, and near-silent operation.

  • Displaces electrically driven chiller load
  • Water refrigerant — zero ODP, no F-gas compliance
  • 25–30 year service life when properly maintained
  • Ideal for CHP, district energy, data centres and process cooling
