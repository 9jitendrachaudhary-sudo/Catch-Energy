Services

Absorption chiller service & maintenance, UK & Europe.

OEM-trained engineers. Planned maintenance, LiBr chemistry, vacuum integrity and 24/7 breakdown response for single and double-effect lithium bromide machines.

Why specialist support matters

Absorption machines need specialists — not general HVAC contractors.

LiBr absorption chillers run under deep vacuum and depend on correct solution chemistry. Generic refrigeration engineers often lack the tools and experience to protect these assets. Catch Energy focuses on absorption plant every day.

Annual & planned maintenance

Structured regimes tailored to machine type, duty and site — from single visits to multi-year contracts with KPI reporting.

LiBr analysis & chemistry

Solution sampling, inhibitor and alkalinity control, crystallisation risk assessment and corrective dosing guidance.

Vacuum testing & leak detection

Integrity checks, non-condensable gas removal and purge system optimisation to maintain design capacity.

24/7 breakdown service

Emergency response for lost cooling — diagnosis, temporary measures and permanent repair.

Commissioning & re-commissioning

New installations and major overhauls brought safely online with performance verification.

Spare parts supply

Genuine and equivalent spares for major absorption chiller brands, sourced across Europe.

Coverage

UK & Europe support.

We support single-effect and double-effect LiBr machines driven by hot water, steam or exhaust gas — in data centres, hospitals, industrial process plants, district energy centres and CHP sites.

  • Planned preventative maintenance contracts
  • Emergency call-out and recovery
  • Performance audits and life-extension recommendations
