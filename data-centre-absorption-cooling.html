Data centre absorption cooling.

Engineering and specialist service for absorption cooling on data centre sites — from feasibility and integration to commissioning and 24/7 support. Lower electrical demand and improve PUE.

The opportunity

Almost all electricity used by IT becomes heat. Absorption puts it back to work.

Modern data centres and AI facilities generate enormous quantities of heat. Where on-site generation or a heat network is available, absorption chillers convert that thermal energy into chilled water with only a fraction of the electrical input of conventional chillers.

Waste heat from on-site power

CHP, engines or turbines produce jacket and exhaust heat that can drive double-effect or direct-fired absorption chillers.

Server exhaust heat export

Low-grade data hall heat can be upgraded with absorption heat pumps and sold to nearby district heating networks.

Grid-constrained sites

When electrical capacity is limited, heat-driven cooling frees megawatts for compute instead of compressors.

Lower PUE & carbon

Reducing chiller electricity demand improves PUE and supports net-zero and ESG reporting.
